# Founder Intelligence: EXIST Grants & BPW Berlin-Brandenburg

*Branch source: `feature/programs-exist-bpw` · Monitored via Horizon Founder Intelligence*

## Executive Summary

This stream tracks official announcements, application rounds, funding guidelines, and winning case benchmarks for two of Germany's most prominent early-stage founder vehicles:
1. **EXIST Program** (Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action / BMWK)
2. **BPW Berlin-Brandenburg** (Businessplan-Wettbewerb Berlin-Brandenburg)

---

## 1. EXIST Program Updates & Application Calls

### EXIST-Gründerstipendium
- **Objective**: Supports university graduates, researchers, and students who want to turn their business idea into a business plan.
- **Grant Terms**:
  - *Postdocs / PhDs*: €3,000/month
  - *Graduates (Master / Diplom)*: €2,500/month
  - *Students (Bachelor / Master students)*: €1,000/month
  - *Team*: Up to 3 members (maximum grant duration: 12 months).
  - *Material Resources*: Up to €30,000 (€10,000 for solo founders) for equipment, software, and consumables.
  - *Coaching Budget*: €5,000 for entrepreneurial guidance and workshops.
- **Application Modality**: Continuous / rolling intake via host university or non-university research institution transfer office.
- **Official Domain**: [exist.de](https://www.exist.de/)

### EXIST-Forschungstransfer
- **Objective**: Funding complex, high-risk development work to prove technical feasibility of research results before spin-off formation.
- **Phase I**: Up to €250,000 + personnel costs for proof of technical feasibility (duration up to 18–24 months).
- **Phase II**: Up to €180,000 non-repayable grant for business launch and product maturity (requires co-financing).

## 2. BPW Berlin-Brandenburg: Winning Cases & 2026/2027 Cycle

The *Businessplan-Wettbewerb Berlin-Brandenburg* is Germany's largest regional startup competition, sponsored by the Senate Department for Economic Affairs (Berlin), the Ministry of Economic Affairs (Brandenburg), and Investitionsbank Berlin (IBB).

### Competition Phases
1. **Phase 1 — Business Model Canvas & Value Proposition**:
   - Focus: Customer segment validation, unique selling proposition (USP), and revenue mechanics.
2. **Phase 2 — Marketing & Sales Architecture**:
   - Focus: Target market size, go-to-market strategy, competitor landscape, and operational team structure.
3. **Phase 3 — Financial Modeling & Investment Readiness**:
   - Focus: 3-year P&L forecast, liquidity planning, break-even analysis, and capitalization table.

### Winning Case Benchmarks & Best Practices
- **Deep Tech & B2B Software**: Teams emphasizing clear API integrations, enterprise security compliance, and demonstrable IP barriers consistently score in top deciles.
- **Life Sciences & Health Tech**: Early regulatory pathway clarification (MDR/IVDR) and clinical validation roadmaps distinguish prize winners.
- **Prize Capital**: Over €50,000 in non-dilutive prize money distributed across the three cycle milestones.

### Upcoming Calls & Events
- Free seminars, peer pitch reviews, and bank consultancy rounds held regularly at IBB and ILB facilities.
- Official registration and deadline calendar: [b-p-w.de](https://www.b-p-w.de/)
